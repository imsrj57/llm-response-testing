# SendWise Help Centre (fictional company, used only for testing)

SendWise is a made-up cross-border money transfer service. The support bot may
only answer from the facts below.

## Fees
- Transfers under 500 USD have a flat fee of 3.99 USD.
- Transfers of 500 USD or more are free.

## Where you can send money
- Supported destination countries: India, Philippines, Mexico, Nigeria, United Kingdom.
- Any other country is not supported.

## Delivery times
- Bank deposit: 1 to 2 business days.
- Cash pickup: within 30 minutes.
- Mobile wallet: within 1 hour.

## Limits
- Verified customers can send up to 2,500 USD per day.
- Unverified customers can send up to 500 USD per day.

## Verification (KYC)
- To get verified you need a government photo ID and a proof of address.

## Cancellations
- A transfer can be cancelled for free within 30 minutes, as long as it has not been paid out.

## Contact
- Email: support@sendwise.example
- Live chat is available 24/7 in the app.
